Behind the Lens

Meet Alex Rivera

What started in 2020 with a secondhand mirrorless camera and a one-way train ticket across the Alps has evolved into a global community of curious explorers.

I started vlogging because traditional travel shows often felt overly scripted and disconnected from reality. My goal with MyVlog is to share honest, cinematic stories—celebrating local street food vendors, remote mountain guides, slow rail journeys, and the quiet moments between destinations.

Interests & Obsessions
